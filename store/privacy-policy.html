Privacy Policy — Straight Talk AI

Last updated: November 14, 2026

Straight Talk AI (“the app”) is an adults-only (18+) advice chat app. Duke is a fictional character who gives blunt, profanity-laced advice for entertainment.

What we collect: nothing

The app does not collect, store, or share any personal information. There is no account, no sign-in, and no server that receives your data.

On-device only

Your chats, your message count, and your OpenAI API key (if you add one) are saved only on your own device, in the app’s local storage. They never leave the device, and deleting the app deletes them. Your API key is sent only to api.openai.com when you ask Duke a question with Real AI on — never to any other server.

No ads, no tracking

The app shows no advertisements, uses no analytics or tracking tools, and contains no links to outside websites.

Purchases

Subscriptions and the lifetime purchase are processed by Apple through In-App Purchase. Apple handles payment; we never see your card details. Purchase status is checked on-device through RevenueCat to unlock the app. RevenueCat receives only the anonymous purchase receipt data needed to verify the subscription — no name or email.

Adults only

This app is for users 18 and older. It contains frequent, intense profanity. It does not knowingly collect any information from anyone under 18.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here and the “Last updated” date above will change.
